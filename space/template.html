HSK level checker

Paste Chinese text to see the HSK level a reader needs: the lowest level at which they know 95% of its characters. "HSK 3.0" names two official documents that put 41.5% of their shared words at different levels, so the result says which one it used, and what the other would say.

Nothing you type leaves your browser. Traditional characters are converted first; the result is then an upper bound.

Built on hsk30 (pip install hsk30, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22234657), from the official syllabus character lists: 3,088 recognition characters in the 2025 syllabus, 3,000 in GF0025-2021. MIT licence. Source: space/ in that repository.
